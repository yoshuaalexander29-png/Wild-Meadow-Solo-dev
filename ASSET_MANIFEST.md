# Asset and provenance manifest — asset-backed iteration

## Hero characters and weapon (external GLB files)

- **Forest wanderer:** Quaternius `Characters_Lis.glb`, loaded from `https://cdn.cinevva.com/assets/packs/quaternius/zombie-apocalypse-kit/Characters_Lis.glb?download=1`.
- **Warrior:** Quaternius `Knight_Male.glb`, loaded from `https://cdn.cinevva.com/assets/packs/quaternius/ultimate-animated-characters/Knight_Male.glb?download=1`.
- **Sword prop:** Quaternius `Sword_Gold.glb`, loaded from `https://cdn.cinevva.com/assets/packs/quaternius/cube-world/Sword_Gold.glb?download=1`.
- Asset catalogue: https://app.cinevva.com/game-assets/free-3d-character-models and https://app.cinevva.com/game-assets/free-3d-weapon-models
- Source creator: Quaternius — https://quaternius.com/
- License stated by the catalogue for these packs: CC0 1.0 Universal. Legal deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The CC0 catalogue pages state the GLBs are downloadable and usable in commercial projects. Keep the exact upstream pack/license metadata with the project and re-check the original asset's license before shipping any redistributed release. Creator credit is included here as provenance even where CC0 does not require it.

## Environment

The meadow terrain, hills, trees, grass, flower patches, lighting, and staging are original code generated by `index.html`. These are environment elements, not substitutes for the two hero models.

## Runtime dependencies

- Three.js `0.183.0` and its GLTFLoader are imported from jsDelivr at runtime.
- Character and sword GLBs are loaded from the Cinevva asset CDN at runtime.
- This is not an offline/self-contained build. Network availability and CDN CORS policy are runtime requirements. Asset load errors are shown in the visible status/error UI.
